About

Statement

I started photographing my own neighborhood on a borrowed camera, mostly to have a reason to stay outside longer. That's still what most of the work is about — staying somewhere a little past the point of comfort, until the ordinary stops looking ordinary.

My work has appeared in a handful of regional publications and two group shows in the Pacific Northwest. I shoot almost entirely on location, in available light, and print everything myself.
